About

More than 30 years generating construction leads.

Construction Leads Network, LLC generates leads for the construction industry. Property owners ask for work. Contractors buy those inquiries either at a fixed price or on commission when they close the job.

The company has more than 30 years of experience in this work. The model has stayed the same: find a real request for construction, and put it in front of a contractor who can do it.

What we are

We are a lead company. We are not a general contractor, and we do not pull permits or send crews. After the lead is delivered, the contractor and the property owner deal with each other.
