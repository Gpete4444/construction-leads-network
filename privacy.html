Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Construction Leads Network, LLC (“we”) handles information when you call, text, email, or use this website. Address: 812 S Blue Ridge Ln, Alpine, UT 84004. Phone: (480) 788-4757. Email: gary@constructionleadsnetwork.com.

Information we collect

If you call, text, or email us about buying leads, we receive your phone number, email, company, trade, and the area you cover. If a property owner uses a website we operate to request construction work, we collect the details they submit, which may include name, phone number, email, project description, and city.

How we use it

We use this information to answer you, to sell per-lead, commission, and flat-fee lead programs to construction companies, and to operate our websites and phone line.

Mobile numbers and text messages

We do not share your mobile phone number with third parties for their own marketing. We do not sell text-message opt-in data.

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

If you opt in to texts from (480) 788-4757, message frequency varies. You may receive up to 4 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel and HELP for help.

A lead we deliver to a construction contractor includes the contact details the property owner submitted so that contractor can respond to the job. That delivery is the service. It is not a sale of text-message consent.

Sharing

We share a property owner’s inquiry with the construction company that bought the lead. We also use phone, email, and website vendors to run the business. We do not sell personal information.
