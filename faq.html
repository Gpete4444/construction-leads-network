FAQ

Questions from contractors.

What does Construction Leads Network sell?

Leads. A lead is a property owner who asked for construction or home-improvement work, with the contact details and the description they gave us.

What is a fixed-price lead?

You pay an agreed price for each lead. The price is set before delivery. You pay it whether or not you win the job.

What is a commission lead?

You pay an agreed share of the job you close from that lead. If you do not close the job, you do not pay a commission on it.

Are the prices on the website?

No. The fixed price and the commission rate depend on the trade and the agreement. Call (480) 788-4757 and we will quote the terms.

Do you guarantee the owner will hire me?

No. We introduce you to someone who asked for work. The hire is between you and the owner.

Are leads shared with other contractors?

That is part of the agreement for your program. Ask when you call. We will tell you, before you start, whether a lead goes to one contractor or more than one.

Which trades can buy leads?

Residential construction and home-improvement trades, including remodeling, roofing, concrete, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, painting, and related work. See the trades list. If yours is missing, call and ask.

How fast does a lead arrive?

We send a lead after the property owner submits the request. We do not promise a daily count.

Do you perform the construction?

No. We do not bid the job, pull the permit, or send a crew.
