Programs

Fixed price, or a commission on the job you close.

Both programs deliver the same kind of lead: a property owner who asked for construction work. The difference is when you pay, and what the fee is tied to.

Fixed-price leads

You and Construction Leads Network agree on a price for each lead before any are delivered. You pay that price for the lead itself. You owe it whether the owner hires you, hires someone else, or does not hire at all.

This fits a contractor who wants a known cost for each inquiry and will make the close on their own.

Commission leads

You pay a share of the construction job you close from the lead. If you do not win the work, you do not pay a commission on it. The rate is agreed before you take leads.

This fits a contractor who wants the fee attached to revenue from that job, not to the inquiry.
