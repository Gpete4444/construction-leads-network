How do I sign up for leads?

Call us. There is no online form. We agree on your trade, your area, and how you will pay before any lead is sent.

Call (480) 788-4757

  1. 1
    Call or email.
    (480) 788-4757 or gary@constructionleadsnetwork.com. Tell us your company name.
  2. 2
    Tell us the work you want.
    Your trade, and the cities or area you cover.
  3. 3
    Choose how you pay.
    A fixed price for each lead, a commission when you close the job, or a flat fee. The flat fee is usually set after a track record of leads and closings.
  4. 4
    Agree on the terms.
    The price or rate is set before the first lead. We do not list prices on this site.
  5. 5
    Leads start.
    A phone call is redirected to your company’s number so you talk with the customer. If no one answers, an email goes to your company’s email. An online inquiry is emailed to your company with the voice call and a transcription of the call.

What to have ready

Your company name, the trade, the area you will travel to, and whether you want per-lead, commission, or flat-fee pricing.
